Nuclei Segmentation (U-Net)

Label every image in a set of fluorescence microscopy images as background, nucleus or nucleus edge. IRIS will train a 2D U-Net on your training pairs and segment each image, recording each run so you can view the labels over the images and compare runs side by side.

Demonstration workflow. This workflow will show what IRIS can do for KGLMF, the Katharina Gaus Light Microscopy Facility. It is a demonstration on public data that did not come from KGLMF, and it has not been validated on the lab's images.
Planned. This workflow has no form yet, so it cannot be run. This page describes what it will do.

What it will take and produce

Description
InputFluorescence microscopy images of nuclei, with some of them labelled to train on, transferred to your scratch.
OutputA label for every pixel of every image, marking background, nucleus or nucleus edge, with the run recorded so you can view the labels over the images and compare runs.

Its data

The demonstration will train on BBBC039, a public collection of fluorescence images of U2OS nuclei. BBBC039's recommended citation is:

We used image set BBBC039v1 Caicedo et al. 2018, available from the Broad Bioimage Benchmark Collection [Ljosa et al., Nature Methods, 2012].

Not settled yet

Its parameters and its environment are settled when this workflow is built.
