# Product logos

The landing page's How IRIS works stages show each product's logo, unlinked, as the product's own file, unaltered. Each project's logo-use terms were read before its logo went in (#224, 2026-11-19).

| Stage | File | Source | Licence and terms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Run | `open-ondemand.png` | Open OnDemand's branding repository, [OSC/ondemand-assests](https://github.com/OSC/ondemand-assests) at `4f7ee49`, `images/2017_1108 Open OnDemand logo/stack/RGB/OpenOnDemand_stack_rev_RGB.png`: the stacked logo's reversed version, white for dark backgrounds, since the colour version's black lettering vanishes on the page | The repository carries no licence file. [openondemand.org/branding](https://openondemand.org/branding) points to it and encourages organisations to use its files in their own presentations and installations. |
| Cite & share | `workflowhub.svg` | WorkflowHub's code, [seek4science/seek](https://github.com/seek4science/seek) at `4883063`, `app/assets/images/logos/workflowhub.svg` | BSD-3-Clause, named in the file's own metadata and the repository's licence; copyright The University of Manchester. WorkflowHub publishes no logo-use terms. |

Two stages show the product's name as text instead:

- **Transfer, Globus**: Globus's [Logo and Trademark Usage Policy](https://www.globus.org/logo-and-trademark) needs the University of Chicago's written permission for the logo. The word "Globus" is fair use.
- **View & compare, MLflow**: MLflow is a mark of LF Projects, whose [trademark policy](https://lfprojects.org/policies/trademark-policy/) needs written permission to show a logo on a website, except as a link to the project's home page, and the stages' logos are not links.
