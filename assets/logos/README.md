# Product logos

The landing page's How IRIS works stages show each product's logo, unlinked, as the product's own file. Open OnDemand's is unaltered; WorkflowHub's has one change, below. Each project's logo-use terms were read before its logo went in (#224, 2026-11-19), and the quoted terms were checked again on 2026-11-19.

Two stages show the product's name as text instead:

- **Transfer, Globus**: Globus's [Logo and Trademark Usage Policy](https://www.globus.org/logo-and-trademark) says "The Globus logo may not be used to imply affiliation with, or endorsement by, the University of Chicago without written permission from University of Chicago", and that its trademarks "may not be used without the written permission of the University of Chicago". For the name it says "The term "Globus" may be used by media, researchers, etc. as covered by fair use, but may not be altered." The stage names Globus, unaltered, as the tool researchers use.
- **View & compare, MLflow**: MLflow is a mark of LF Projects, whose [trademark policy](https://lfprojects.org/policies/trademark-policy/) needs written permission to show a logo on a website, except as a link to the project's home page, and the stages' logos are not links.
